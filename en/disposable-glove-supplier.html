The gloves we supply

Each type answers a different use

Nitrile gloves

The most common choice in food, medical and industrial settings. Latex-free, resistant to tearing and to many chemicals. Available in several thicknesses, powdered or powder-free.

Vinyl gloves

An economical solution for short, low-risk tasks: food service, light cleaning, handling with no chemical contact.

Latex gloves

Good dexterity and a precise fit. To be avoided wherever latex allergies are possible.

Handling gloves

Reusable gloves for the warehouse, the shop and maintenance, where a disposable glove is not enough.

How to choose the right glove

Three questions are enough in most cases

1. What contact?

Food, chemicals, patient care, handling: the use determines the material and the type of glove.

2. What thickness?

The thicker the glove, the longer it lasts, but the less dexterity it offers. The right balance depends on how long it is worn.

3. What volume?

Unit price depends directly on the quantities ordered. That is often where the savings are.
