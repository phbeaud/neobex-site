Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information

1. Introduction

Neobex ("we", "our") takes the protection of your personal information seriously. This policy describes how we collect, use, disclose and retain information obtained through your use of our website and services.

2. Information we collect

We may collect the following:

  • Identity and contact information (first and last name, business email, phone, company name) submitted through our quote and contact forms
  • Details of your request (product category, estimated volumes, message)
  • Technical browsing data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) collected automatically

3. Purposes of use

Your information is used to:

  • Respond to your quote, information or contact requests
  • Prepare and send customized quotes
  • Improve our product and service offerings
  • Comply with our legal and regulatory obligations

4. Information sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share it with:

  • Our technology service providers (web hosting, form processing) under confidentiality agreements
  • Competent authorities when required by law

5. Retention and security

We retain your information for the duration necessary for the intended purpose and in accordance with our legal obligations. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss or disclosure.

6. Your rights

Under applicable laws, you can exercise the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access and review
  • Rectification or update
  • Withdrawal of consent
  • Deletion (subject to legal obligations)
  • Portability

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@neobex.ca.

7. Cookies

Our site may use cookies to ensure proper operation and analyze usage. You can configure your browser to refuse them, but this may affect some features.

8. Policy changes

We reserve the right to modify this policy. The last update date is shown at the top of the page. Changes take effect upon publication on this page.
