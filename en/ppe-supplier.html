Badly chosen protective equipment does not protect: it gets worn crooked, taken off too early or replaced too late. That is why we start by understanding your workstations before proposing products.

Neobex supplies personal protective equipment to more than 500 Canadian businesses: masks, safety glasses, face shields, disposable garments and gloves. Our clients often arrive with three or four different suppliers for a single site. Our job is to bring that back to a single list, with pricing that reflects the combined volumes.

How it works

Three steps, with no commitment on your part

1. You send your list

Invoices, purchase orders or a simple product list. The format does not matter: we work with whatever you have on hand.

2. We analyze it

We identify the equivalents, check formats and quantities, then compare against what you pay today.

3. You receive a quote

A clear proposal, product by product, with free samples if you want to check before changing anything.
