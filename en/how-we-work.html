How we work

What actually happens between the first call and the first delivery.

Most suppliers send you a catalogue and wait for you to find what you need in it. We do the opposite: we start from what you already buy, and work from there.

This page describes the whole process, including what we ask of you and what we do not do. If you have just spoken with us on the phone and you are wondering what working with us looks like, this is it.

The five steps

From the first document to recurring delivery

1

You send us what you already buy

An invoice, a purchase order, an Excel file, or a photo of the box taken in your warehouse. The format does not matter in the slightest.

We start there rather than making you work through a catalogue of 2,000 items, because it is the only way to compare like with like. A price only means something when it is tied to the exact product, the exact format and the exact quantity you go through.

What it asks of you: five minutes and a document you already have on hand.
2

We analyze it, line by line

For each item we identify what we can supply: the same product, or an equivalent where it genuinely is one. We check formats, volumes and quantities per case, because that is often where the apparent price gaps between two suppliers are hiding.

When an item has no satisfactory equivalent in our range, we say so. Sometimes we recommend keeping a product with your current supplier.

What we do not do: swap a product for a cheaper but weaker one without telling you. A glove that has to be changed twice as often saves nobody anything.
3

You test before changing anything

For any product your teams use daily, we send free samples. A technical data sheet tells you what a product is supposed to do; a week of real use tells you whether it does it at your workstations.

This is the step buyers skip most often, and the one that causes the most returns.

What it asks of you: have the product tried by the people who wear or use it, not only by purchasing.
4

You receive a quote, product by product

Not a lump sum, but one line per item: what you pay today, what we propose, and the matching format. You see immediately where the gaps are, and you can take only part of it.

Unit price depends on volume. So we show you what the box, the case and the pallet each give, so you decide knowing the brackets rather than discovering them afterwards.

No commitment: you can take two items out of fifteen, or none at all. The analysis stays yours either way.
5

We deliver, and we keep your stock available

The products you go through regularly are kept in inventory to avoid stockouts, because running out of gloves or hairnets stops a production line — and at that point the unit price no longer matters at all.

We provide the technical data sheets your quality teams need for their compliance files and their audits.
